How Tcash works

Updated 6 October 2026 · M-Pesa Kenya

Buy WLD or USDC

Choose an asset and the KES amount to pay. Review the saved crypto amount, Tcash fee, PayBill and account number before paying. Submit the 10-character M-Pesa code from your confirmation SMS. An operator checks the payment and sends crypto to your World wallet.

Sell for M-Pesa

Enter the crypto amount and confirm your own Kenyan M-Pesa number. Review the saved payout and fee, then approve World Pay. If a manual transfer is offered, use only the saved receiving address on World Chain and submit the full transaction hash.

Rates and fees

The first estimate may change when Tcash saves your order. The saved amounts stay with that order. M-Pesa may charge separately. Check its confirmation screen before approving.

Track your order

Pending means payment information is still needed. Reviewing means your payment was submitted and awaits operator checks. Settled means the operator has recorded completion. Settlement is manual. If delayed, contact support with your order ID. Never pay twice for the same order.

Eligibility and risks

You must be 18 or older and use your own World wallet and registered M-Pesa Kenya account. Provide accurate details and use only funds you own. Crypto prices can change and assets can lose value. Tcash does not provide investment advice or promise returns.
